## 3. AI Rebranding Workflow (9 Steps, 4-12 Hours Per Product)

### Step 1: License Audit & Product Selection (0.5-1 hr)

- Download the PLR product and verify editable source files are included (Canva link, DOCX, or raw text)
- Read the license file: confirm resale rights, check for minimum price clauses, platform restrictions
- Verify the license allows the format transformation you plan (e.g., ebook → Notion dashboard)

### Step 2: Audience & Angle Analysis (0.5-1 hr)

- Pick a **specific niche audience** with a **specific pain point** and **specific transformation promise**
- Research what's currently selling on Gumroad in that niche using InsightRaider/InfoProdSpy
- Use Pinterest and Etsy bestseller searches to identify trending sub-niches
- **Prompt for gap analysis (ChatGPT):**
  > "Here is a PLR product on [topic]. It currently includes: [list assets]. Analyze this product and identify gaps, missing elements, or weak areas compared to what is currently selling. Then suggest specific improvements, additional assets, or upgrades I can add to make this product more valuable, more complete, and better than competing versions."

**Example transformation:** Generic "Social Media Marketing 101" → "The 30-Day Instagram Growth Blueprint for Introverted Content Creators"

### Step 3: Brand Voice Engineering (0.5-1 hr)

- Create a **Brand Voice Print**: 2-3 writing style samples, structural rules (max 3-line paragraphs, active voice, no passive voice), banned AI words ("delve," "embark," "unlock," "leverage")
- Define target persona: age, profession, pain point, desired outcome
- This Voice Print becomes a reusable style anchor for all products in your store

### Step 4: Content Deconstruction & Fact Update (1-2 hrs)

- Feed the PLR content into Claude (large context window) and extract a raw fact sheet
- Strip transitions, fluff, and dated examples
- Reorder facts around a new logical angle (30-day roadmap, diagnostic framework, step-by-step checklist)
- **Prompt for fact modernization (ChatGPT with search):**
  > "Scan this raw fact sheet and challenge every date, claim, and data point. Replace outdated statistics and tool recommendations with current 2026 data, citing 'according to recent market evaluations published in Q1 2026' where appropriate."

### Step 5: Content Rewrite & Humanization (1.5-3 hrs)

Use a **layered, multi-pass process** with ChatGPT:

**Pass 1 — Niche voice transplant:**
  > "Analyze the tone, sentence structure, and vocabulary of these [2-3 writing samples]. Now, rewrite this PLR chapter using this exact identity."

**Pass 2 — Surgical section rewrite:**
  > "Rewrite this for [Ideal Client] who struggles with [Pain Point]. Use casual, punchy sentences under 15 words. Add 1 relatable metaphor."

**Pass 3 — Anti-AI-ism cleanup:**
  > "Review this rewritten PLR article and identify any sentences that sound robotic, vague, or too formal. Rewrite those sections to be useful and human. Add practical examples where the original is abstract."

### Step 6: Visual Redesign in Canva (1-3 hrs)

- Set up a **Brand Kit** in Canva Pro: 2-3 colors, 1-2 fonts (consistent across all products)
- Use Canva's **Magic Design** to generate original cover concepts
- Redesign interior pages for visual consistency
- Build **4-5 product mockups** (this is critical — going from 1 cover to 4-5 covers multiplies average revenue by 18x)
- Swap stock photos to match your niche theme

### Step 7: Format Transformation (0.5-3 hrs)

Choose the highest-value format for your niche:

| Transformation | Effort | Value Multiplier | Best For |
|---------------|--------|-----------------|----------|
| Ebook → Email course | Low | High | Lead magnet + funnel entry |
| Ebook → Notion dashboard | Medium | High | Productivity/professional niches ($25-$49) |
| PDF → Canva template | Medium | High | Social media/design niches ($14-$40) |
| Manual → Interactive checklist | Low | Medium | Lead magnet or low-ticket upsell |
| Single product → Bundle | Low | Highest | Moving $15 product to $49-$79 price point |

**Prompt for ebook → email course (ChatGPT):**
  > "Turn the following PLR chapter into the third email of a 5-part email course. The audience is [audience], the promise is [promise], and the CTA is [CTA]. Keep the tone [tone]. Add a subject line under 10 words and a one-sentence CTA at the end."

**Prompt for ebook → Notion dashboard (Claude):**
  > "I am turning the following ebook content into a Notion dashboard. Based on the chapter structure below, design a Notion database schema. For each property, choose the correct Notion property type: title, rich_text, select, multi_select, number, date, checkbox, url, relation, rollup. Provide a JSON-like schema with property names and types, and a one-line description of what each property should track. Keep the schema to 5-7 properties to start."

### Step 8: Listing Copy & SEO Tags (0.5-1 hr)

See **Section 4: Listing Copy Templates** below for exact templates.

### Step 9: Publish & Promote (0.5-2 hrs)

- Upload to Gumroad (primary) and Payhip (secondary — 5% fee vs 10%)
- Set tiered pricing (see Section 5)
- Schedule launch: post in 2-3 relevant niche subreddits (problem-solution format, not self-promo), create 5-10 Pinterest pins, share in 1-2 Discord/Facebook niche communities
- Add "30-day money back guarantee" in bold (9.9x revenue multiplier)

## 5. Pricing Strategy

### Platform Sweet Spots

| Niche | Entry Price | Core Price | Premium/Bundle |
|-------|------------|------------|----------------|
| ChatGPT prompt libraries | $9.99 (PWYW) | $29 | $49-$97 |
| Budget/finance planners | $12 | $25 | $47 (Notion bundle) |
| Content creator toolkits | $10 (minimal) | $29-$49 (full OS) | $79+ (agency) |
| Social media templates | $14 (medium pack) | $25-$35 (large pack) | $39-$45 (complete kit) |
| Email marketing templates | $12 | $27 | $47 (mega bundle) |

### Pricing Rules

1. **Charm pricing:** End prices in .99 or .97 ($19.99, $29.97). Avoid odd non-standard endings like $23.
2. **Tiered pricing (2-3 tiers):** Generates 2x revenue vs single-tier. Format:
   - Starter: $19 (basic version)
   - **Pro: $29 (MOST POPULAR — 70% choose this)**
   - Premium: $49 (with bonuses + MRR rights)
3. **Avoid the $10-19 death zone:** 40% of products but only 5.5% of revenue. Either go below $10 (impulse buy/lead magnet) or above $19.
4. **Avoid the $50-99 dead zone in Business & Money:** Average 7.1 sales (worse than $1-49 tier at 8.1 sales). Jump straight from $49 to $100+.
5. **Bundle multiplier:** Single PLR product at $15 → bundle 3-5 products → sell at $49-$79. Bundling is the highest-value transformation.
6. **MRR premium:** Products with MRR rights command 40-80% higher prices. A $12 planner without MRR → $17-$27 with MRR.
7. **Price anchoring:** Display "Original Value $150" next to "Your Price $29". Stack bonuses: Main ($49) + Bonus 1 ($19) + Bonus 2 ($29) + Bonus 3 ($39) = $136 value → your price $49.

### Platform Fee Comparison

| Platform | Fee on $29 Sale | Best For |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| Gumroad | $3.40 (10% + $0.50) + processing | Primary — built-in audience, Google ranking |
| Payhip | $1.45 (5%) | Secondary — lower fees, list same products |

**Strategy:** List on both platforms simultaneously (non-exclusive). Gumroad for discovery + Google SEO. Payhip for direct links where you keep more margin.

## 7. Risk Mitigation

### Platform Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Gumroad PLR policy** — prohibits unmodified PLR reselling, account suspension risk | Always complete the full 9-step rebranding workflow. Never upload raw PLR. Add original tutorials, bonuses, or format transformations. |
| **Etsy Creativity Standards** — June 2025 update bans PLR reselling without substantial reworking | **Do not list on Etsy in Month 1.** Focus on Gumroad + Payhip. If expanding to Etsy later, require full content + visual transformation + process documentation. |
| **License violations** — minimum price clauses, platform restrictions | Read every product's license file before rebranding. Keep a license compliance log. Check for min price ($7+), no Kindle/eBay, no free giveaway restrictions. |
| **Market saturation** — identical PLR products competing | Specificity is the only differentiator. Rebrand to narrow niches. "Email templates for wedding photographers" vs "email templates." |
| **Gumroad algorithm invisibility** — new sellers may not appear in Discover | Drive external traffic (Pinterest, Reddit, niche communities). Gumroad product pages rank on Google — write 300+ word descriptions with target keywords. |

### Revenue Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Median Gumroad product earns ~$2** | This is the median for all products. Products with 6,000+ char descriptions, 4-5 covers, tiered pricing, and guarantees earn significantly more. Follow the listing optimization checklist exactly. |
| **42% of Gumroad sales come from email** — zero-audience sellers are disadvantaged | Use PWYW pricing for first product to accumulate ratings (2.6x rating advantage). Build email list from Gumroad buyer emails. Add free lead magnet (checklist/template) to capture emails. |
| **$10-19 death zone** | Price below $10 (impulse/lead magnet) or above $19. Never price in the $10-19 range. |

### Operational Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **AI content detection** | Use the multi-pass humanization workflow. Remove AI-isms ("delve," "embark," "unlock," "leverage"). Add personal examples and opinions. Have a human review pass. |
| **Canva Pro element licensing** | Only use free Canva elements in templates shared with buyers. Or use locked-PNG + editable text boxes workaround. |
| **Time overrun** | Standard rebrand is 4-12 hrs per product. Start with simpler products (email templates, social media templates) before tackling complex transformations (Notion dashboards, courses). |
