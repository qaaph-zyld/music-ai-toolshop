# Research Findings: Digital Arbitrage & Flipping Strategies with $0–$100 Starting Capital (2025–2026)

## Scope
- **Question:** What digital arbitrage or flipping strategies are viable with $0–$100 starting capital in 2025–2026? Include domain flipping, digital template reselling, PLR products, Notion template arbitrage, Figma/UI kit flipping, and AI-assisted arbitrage methods. What are realistic profit margins, time requirements, and platform risks?
- **Boundaries:** Included — domain flipping economics, PLR product reselling, template arbitrage (Notion, Figma, Canva), AI-generated asset flipping, marketplace fees and rules. Excluded — crypto arbitrage, physical product flipping, dropshipping with inventory.

## 1. Domain Flipping Economics

### Key Findings

- **Entry cost:** A standard `.com` domain costs $8–$15/year to register. With $100, you can acquire 6–12 domains at registration cost. ([Daily Thrive](https://thedailythrive.net/lifestyle/how-much-does-astroflipping-cost/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Renewal trap:** Domains expire annually. If unsold, renewal fees create a recurring cost burden. 20 domains at $12 each = $240/year in renewals alone. ([Daily Thrive](https://thedailythrive.net/lifestyle/how-much-does-astroflipping-cost/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Marketplace commissions:** Sedo, Afternic, and Dan charge 10–15% commission on sales. GoDaddy Auctions charges $5–$15 listing fees plus success fees. ([Daily Thrive](https://thedailythrive.net/lifestyle/how-much-does-astroflipping-cost/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Tool costs:** Premium research tools (DropCatch, ExpiredDomains.net) run $20–$60/month ($240–$720/year), which exceeds a $100 budget. Free alternatives exist but are limited. ([Daily Thrive](https://thedailythrive.net/lifestyle/how-much-does-astroflipping-cost/) — accessed 2026-10-10)
- **Realistic ROI:** A $10 domain might sell for $100–$1,000, but most domains never sell. The "loss leader" strategy — liquidating underperformers at a loss to fund premium acquisitions — is recommended for portfolio optimization. ([Domavest](https://www.domavest.com/2026/03/the-loss-leader-strategy-liquidating.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Time requirement:** ~8 hours/week minimum for research, listing, and outreach. At minimum wage, this is ~$80/week in opportunity cost. ([Daily Thrive](https://thedailythrive.net/lifestyle/how-much-does-astroflipping-cost/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **New gTLDs:** `.ai`, `.io`, `.co`, `.xyz` domains hold value for tech startups but are more speculative. `.com` remains the most valuable TLD. ([4TheLowtho](https://4thelowtho.com/digital-land-banking-strategic-acquisition-for-internet-equity/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Platform Risks
- **Valuation tools are unreliable:** Estibot and GoDaddy appraisal tools are algorithmic estimates, not buyer offers. Paying $30 for appraisal reports is generally not worthwhile. ([Daily Thrive](https://thedailythrive.net/lifestyle/how-much-does-astroflipping-cost/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Trademark risk:** Must verify domains aren't trademarked by existing companies. ([Daily Thrive](https://thedailythrive.net/lifestyle/how-much-does-astroflipping-cost/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Holding cost erosion:** Renewal fees on unsold domains erode capital over time. A $10 domain held for 2 years costs $20 before any sale. ([Domavest](https://www.domavest.com/2026/03/the-loss-leader-strategy-liquidating.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Summary Table

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Minimum capital | $8–$15 per domain |
| Realistic with $100 | 6–12 domains |
| Commission fees | 10–15% per sale |
| Time requirement | 8+ hrs/week |
| Profit margin (if sold) | 100–9,900% (but most domains never sell) |
| Key risk | Renewal costs, low sell-through rate |

## 2. PLR (Private Label Rights) Product Reselling

### Key Findings

- **What PLR is:** PLR licenses allow you to modify, rebrand, and claim authorship of digital products (ebooks, templates, courses). You keep 100% of profits. ([Digital Biz PLR](https://digitalbizplr.com/blogs/learn/how-to-sell-plr-products) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Startup cost:** Quality PLR products cost $10–$50 each. Some platforms (IDPLR.com, PLR.me) offer free samples or limited free memberships. ([Sellfy Blog](https://sellfy.com/blog/resell-white-label-digital-products/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Pricing strategy:** Customized PLR products sell for $7–$97 depending on type:
  - Social media templates: $7–$27
  - Digital planners: $15–$47
  - Ebooks/guides: $17–$37
  - Course bundles: $47–$97
  ([Digital Biz PLR](https://digitalbizplr.com/blogs/learn/how-to-sell-plr-products) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **The golden rule:** Do not sell PLR as-is. Rebranding, reformatting (e.g., ebook → email course, manual → Notion dashboard), and retitling to niche audiences is essential for premium pricing. A generic "Social Media Marketing 101" becomes "The 30-Day Instagram Growth Blueprint for Introverted Content Creators." ([PLR Digital Club](https://plrdigitalclub.com/blog/reselling-plr-products-2026-guide) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **License types:** PLR (most freedom — edit, rebrand, resell), MRR (Master Resell Rights — can pass resale rights to customers but limited modification), RR (Resell Rights — single-tier resale only). ([Post Affiliate Pro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/faq/can-plr-content-be-resold/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **License restrictions:** Some PLR licenses include minimum/maximum price points (e.g., cannot sell below $7 or above $97), restrictions on selling on Amazon Kindle or eBay, and prohibitions on giving away for free. ([Post Affiliate Pro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/faq/can-plr-content-be-resold/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Platform Risks

- **Etsy PLR crackdown (June 2025):** Etsy's updated Creativity Standards require digital downloads to be the seller's *original design*. Buying PLR/clipart and reselling — even with a commercial license — does not qualify as "Designed by you." Superficial changes (swapping fonts, changing colors) are insufficient. Substantial reworking (rewriting content, redesigning layout, building new visual identity) is required. Listings violating this are being removed. ([The PLR Store](https://www.theplrstore.com/what-etsys-2025-policy-changes-mean-for-plr-and-digital-sellers.html) — accessed 2026-08-10; [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Etsy is an Etsy-specific issue:** Gumroad, Payhip, Shopify, and own-website sales have no such restriction. PLR content remains fully viable on these platforms. ([The PLR Store](https://www.theplrstore.com/what-etsys-2025-policy-changes-mean-for-plr-and-digital-sellers.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Saturation risk:** Unmodified PLR creates identical competing products. Customization is the only differentiator. ([Boundless PLR](https://shop.boundlessplr.com/blogs/latest-tips-for-digital-sellers/how-to-resell-plr-digital-products) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Selling Platforms

| Platform | Fee | Best For |
|----------|-----|----------|
| Gumroad | 10% + $0.50 per sale | Fast launch, link-in-bio |
| Payhip | 5% (free plan) | Low-cost, keeping margin |
| Etsy | 6.5% + $0.20 listing + processing | Discovery (but PLR risk) |
| Shopify | $39/mo + payment fees | Established businesses |
| Stan Store | $29–$99/mo + Stripe fees | Link-in-bio, mobile-first |

([PLR Digital Club](https://plrdigitalclub.com/blog/reselling-plr-products-2026-guide) — accessed 2026-08-10; [Boundless PLR](https://shop.boundlessplr.com/blogs/latest-tips-for-digital-sellers/how-to-resell-plr-digital-products) — accessed 2026-08-10)

## 3. Notion Template Arbitrage

### Key Findings

- **Market state (2026):** Generic productivity templates are saturated (50,000+ Etsy listings for "Notion student planner" with 500–2,000+ reviews on top results). Niche/profession-specific templates remain open: "Notion template for therapists" has only 8–45 reviews on top listings. ([Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/is-selling-notion-templates-still-profitable-2026) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Realistic earnings:** Most sellers who build consistently for 6–12 months earn $500–$3,000/month. Median seller with one template and no distribution makes close to nothing. Top sellers (Thomas Frank) report $1M+ in annual revenue. ([Grey Journal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-to-sell-notion-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Profit math:** At a $29 price point on Payhip's 5% plan, you keep ~$26.55 after fees. To reach $1,000/month, you need ~38 sales/month (~1.5/day), requiring several hundred people finding your listing weekly. ([Grey Journal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-to-sell-notion-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Niche opportunities (2026):** Healthcare professionals (nurses, therapists, OTs), creative freelancers (photographers, videographers), real estate agents, life-event finance trackers (divorce, new homeowner, wedding), ADHD/neurodivergent planning systems. Buyers in these niches pay $25–$79 for professional-grade systems. ([Kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/is-selling-notion-templates-still-profitable-2026) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Bundling strategy:** Thomas Frank's Creator's Companion + Ultimate Brain bundle outsold individual editions 3:1 and pushed average order value up. Build 3 related templates at $19–$29 each, sell the set at $49. ([Grey Journal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-to-sell-notion-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Quality bar raised:** Notion now ships AI agents and AI meeting notes natively. A page of empty databases isn't a product anymore. Templates must have opinionated structure, prefilled examples, and documentation that teaches a workflow. ([Grey Journal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-to-sell-notion-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Platform Fees Comparison

| Platform | Fee Per Sale | Built-in Traffic | Best For |
|----------|-------------|------------------|----------|
| Notion Marketplace | 10% + $0.40 | High (Notion users) | Discovery, credibility |
| Etsy | 6.5% + $0.20 listing + processing | High (search-driven) | Passive marketplace traffic |
| Gumroad | 10% + $0.50 (direct); 30% (Discover) | Low | Fast launch |
| Payhip | 5% (free plan) | Low | Keeping margin at volume |
| Lemon Squeezy | 5% + $0.50 | None | VAT handling |
| Latuos | 2% + Stripe processing | None | Own audience, direct payouts |

([Notion Help Center](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace) — accessed 2026-08-10; [Latuos](https://latuos.com/sell-notion-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10; [Grey Journal](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-to-sell-notion-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Platform Risks
- **Notion Marketplace approval:** Requires waitlist approval and Stripe onboarding. 14-day fund hold. $20 minimum payout threshold. 1% foreign exchange fee for non-US creators. ([Notion Help Center](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Notion Marketplace fee workaround:** List on Marketplace for discovery but link to a third-party checkout (Payhip, Gumroad) to avoid the 10% + $0.40 fee. Notion explicitly supports this. ([Payhip Blog](https://payhip.com/blog/how-to-sell-notion-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Etsy PLR risk:** Reselling PLR Notion templates on Etsy risks removal under June 2025 Creativity Standards. ([Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards) — accessed 2026-08-10)

## 4. Figma/UI Kit Flipping

### Key Findings

- **Market size:** The design asset market is growing at 35% annually, reaching $2.1B in 2026. ([EarnifyHub](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/ui-ux-design-asset-sales-figma-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Realistic income:** Designers who commit consistently report $500–$3,000/month within 6–12 months from a catalog of 5–15 products. Most template authors make $0–$500/month in their first year. ([SellRamp](https://sellramp.com/blog/how-to-sell-figma-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10; [AI Study Room](https://aidev.fit/en/sidehustle/sell-website-templates.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Pricing (2026):**
  - Single-use social media template: $9–$19
  - Presentation template (20–40 slides): $19–$49
  - Wireframe/flowchart kit (100+ components): $29–$79
  - Dashboard template (10–20 charts): $39–$99
  - Full UI kit or design system: $79–$199
  - Comprehensive design system: $199–$499
  ([SellRamp](https://sellramp.com/blog/how-to-sell-figma-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10; [AI Study Room](https://aidev.fit/en/sidehustle/sell-website-templates.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Top sellers' revenue:** Figma UI Kits: $5K–$15K/month for top sellers. Dashboard templates: $4K–$12K. Design systems: $6K–$20K. These are top performers, not averages. ([EarnifyHub](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/ui-ux-design-asset-sales-figma-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Framework-specific kits command premium:** SaaS boilerplates (Next.js + Supabase + Stripe) sell for $79–$299 because they save developers 20–40 hours of setup. ([AI Study Room](https://aidev.fit/en/sidehustle/sell-website-templates.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Maintenance is critical:** Templates not updated in 6 months get bad reviews and stop selling. 40% of time goes to maintenance/support, 30% to marketing, 30% to new products. ([AI Study Room](https://aidev.fit/en/sidehustle/sell-website-templates.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)

|-------------|---------------------------|----------|----------|
| Figma Community | 85% (15% fee) | Figma users | Direct in-app sales |
| Creative Market | 70% (30% fee) | Design professionals | UI kits, design assets |
| ThemeForest (Envato) | 50–62.5% | Largest buyer base | WordPress, HTML themes |
| Webflow Templates | 70% | Webflow designers | Webflow templates |

| Gumroad | 90% (10% + $0.30) | Your audience | Direct sales |

([Figma Help Center](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/12067637274519-About-selling-Community-resources) — accessed 2026-08-10; [TemplateTrip](https://www.templatetrip.com/blog/top-websites-to-sell-figma-templates-2026) — accessed 2026-08-10; [AI Study Room](https://aidev.fit/en/sidehustle/sell-website-templates.html) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Platform Risks
- **Figma Community refund policy:** Customers can request automatic refunds within 24 hours of one-time purchases, charged back to the seller. ([Figma Help Center](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/12067637274519-About-selling-Community-resources) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Figma payout timing:** Cash out only 30 US business days after a sale, max once per week. ([Figma Help Center](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/12067637274519-About-selling-Community-resources) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **High commission marketplaces:** Envato Elements uses a subscription revenue-share pool — per-unit earnings are lower than individual sales. UI8 charges 30–40% commission. ([TemplateTrip](https://www.templatetrip.com/blog/top-websites-to-sell-figma-templates-2026) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Figma Community free tier:** Publishing free templates generates visibility but $0 revenue. Useful as a funnel to paid listings elsewhere. ([SellRamp](https://sellramp.com/blog/how-to-sell-figma-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Capital Requirement
- **With $0:** Can design in Figma's free tier (3 files). Sell on Gumroad (no monthly fee, 10% per sale). Limited to 3 active design files.
- **With $100:** Figma Professional ($15/month) unlocks unlimited files. Remaining $85 covers ~5 months. List on multiple marketplaces simultaneously (non-exclusive).

## 5. Canva Template Reselling

### Key Findings

- **Entry cost:** Canva Pro required to create shareable template links — $15/month or $120/year. A single $15 template sale covers the monthly cost. Canva Free can design but cannot share template links. ([CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-canva-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Realistic earnings:** Most beginners earn $0–$500/month. Niche sellers with strong delivery report $2,000–$6,000/month part-time on Etsy alone. ([CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-canva-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Pricing (2026):**
  - Single template: $5–$15
  - Niche bundle: $29–$79
  - Mega bundle: $99–$199
  - Niche white-glove packs: $99–$249
  ([SellRamp](https://sellramp.com/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10; [Quickshops](https://quickshops.app/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **License restriction:** Templates with Canva Pro elements violate Canva's license when the buyer is on a Free account. Must use free elements only, or use the locked-PNG plus editable text boxes workaround. ([CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-canva-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Canva Creator program:** Application-based, royalty-only (Canva sets rates based on "Editable Template Uses"). No price control. Rejection is possible. ([CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-canva-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Platform Fees

| Platform | Fee on $10 Sale | Best For |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| Etsy | ~$1.80 (6.5% + $0.20 + processing) | Marketplace discovery |
| Gumroad | $1.50 (10% + $0.50) + processing | Fast launch |
| Payhip | $0.50 (5%) | Low-cost margin |
| Latuos | ~$0.49 (2% + Stripe) | Own audience |
| Sellfy | $0 (subscription: $22–$29/mo) | Storefront control |

([Latuos](https://latuos.com/sell-canva-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10; [Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/blog/sell-canva-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Platform Risks
- **Etsy saturation:** Generic Canva templates are heavily saturated. Niche-specific packs (real estate, wedding, healthcare) perform better. ([SellRamp](https://sellramp.com/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Etsy PLR/Creativity Standards:** Reselling Canva templates you didn't design risks removal. Must be original designs. ([Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Canva Pro element licensing:** Using Pro elements in templates shared with Free users violates Canva's Content License Agreement. ([CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-canva-templates/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

## 6. AI-Generated Asset Flipping

### Key Findings

- **AI stock video arbitrage:** Generate cinematic footage using Kling 3.0 ($0.10 in API compute per shot), upscale to 4K with Topaz Video AI, and distribute via Wirestock across Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Getty, and Pond5. Wirestock handles keyword tagging and syndication, splitting royalties with the creator. ([Big AI Reports](https://bigaireports.com/ai-asset-arbitrage-flux-loras-kling/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **LORA training (high-ticket):** Rent cloud GPUs on RunPod at ~$0.44/hour, train custom Flux/Stable Diffusion LORAs for e-commerce brands using 20 product photos. Sell model weights directly to Shopify owners for $500–$1,500 per LORA. ([Big AI Reports](https://bigaireports.com/ai-asset-arbitrage-flux-loras-kling/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **PromptBase (AI prompt marketplace):**
  - 20% marketplace commission (80% to seller). 0% when buyer comes via your referral link.
  - Median prompt price: $5.99. 91% of prompts priced at $10 or under.
  - Minimum listing price: $1.99. No listing fees.
  - Payouts: Weekly via PayPal or Stripe. $30 minimum balance for Stripe payout.
  - Realistic earnings: $0–$50/month 1; $50–$200/month 2–3; $150–$500/month 3–6; $300–$1,500+/month at 6+ months with 30–50+ listings.
  ([PromptBase](https://promptbase.com/sell) — accessed 2026-08-10; [AI Profit Mode](https://aiprofitmode.com/how-to-sell-on-promptbase.html) — accessed 2026-08-10; [CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-ai-prompts/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **AI-generated art on Etsy:** Etsy allows seller-prompted AI creations under "Designed by a seller" — but the listing must disclose AI use, and the design must reflect genuine original work. ([Etsy Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/ca/legal/creativity) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **AI slop economy (2026):** The market is flooded with AI-generated content. A Midjourney subscription producing 1,000 renders/month costs less than a single stock illustration commission. The defensible position has shifted from "I can produce more" to "I produce things AI cannot easily." ([Sailop](https://sailop.com/blog/ai-slop-economy-2026) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Digital product arbitrage with AI:** A $5 PLR product can be improved with AI-assisted customization and resold as a $79 bundle. A $10 Notion template can become a $250 productivity system with AI-enhanced documentation. ([Kurt Tasche](https://kurttasche.com/digital-product-arbitrage-the-underground-method-turning-5-downloads-into-300-sales/) — accessed 2026-08-10)

### Capital Requirements for AI Arbitrage

| Method | Minimum Capital | Tools Needed |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| AI prompt selling (PromptBase) | $0 | PromptBase account (free) |
| AI stock video | ~$20–$50 | Kling API credits, Topaz Video AI ($299 one-time or trial), Wirestock (free) |
| LORA training | ~$10–$20 | RunPod GPU rental ($0.44/hr × ~20 hrs), Kohya_ss (free) |
| AI art on Etsy | $0 + Midjourney $10/mo | Midjourney subscription, Etsy listing fees ($0.20 each) |
| AI-enhanced PLR flipping | $10–$50 | PLR product + ChatGPT/Claude (free tiers) |

### Platform Risks
- **Stock agency QA:** Adobe Stock and Shutterstock have strict quality assurance algorithms. Low-resolution, artifact-heavy AI content is rejected. Temporal consistency issues (background warping) are the #1 rejection reason. ([Big AI Reports](https://bigaireports.com/ai-asset-arbitrage-flux-loras-kling/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Market saturation:** AI-generated content volume is exploding. Individual artists competing on volume will lose to automated pipelines. ([Sailop](https://sailop.com/blog/ai-slop-economy-2026) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **PromptBase payout delays:** 72-hour pending balance, then $30 minimum for Stripe payout. Early payout costs $3.99 (eats 83% of a median sale's earnings). ([CartMango](https://cartmango.com/sell-ai-prompts/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **IP/licensing uncertainty:** AI-generated content has unresolved legal questions about training-data provenance and IP ownership. ([Sailop](https://sailop.com/blog/ai-slop-economy-2026) — accessed 2026-08-10)

## 7. Digital Product Arbitrage (General Flipping Strategy)

### Key Findings

- **Core concept:** Find undervalued digital assets, improve them, repackage, and sell at significantly higher prices. A $5 product becomes a $79 bundle. A $10 template becomes a $250 productivity system. ([Kurt Tasche](https://kurttasche.com/digital-product-arbitrage-the-underground-method-turning-5-downloads-into-300-sales/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Value multiplication methods:**
  - Rebranding (new title, cover, visual identity)
  - Improving presentation (professional mockups, better copy)
  - Creating bundles (combine several related items into a complete toolkit)
  - Changing format (ebook → email course, manual → Notion dashboard, text → video)
  ([Kurt Tasche](https://kurttasche.com/digital-product-arbitrage-the-underground-method-turning-5-downloads-into-300-sales/) — accessed 2026-08-10; [PLR Digital Club](https://plrdigitalclub.com/blog/reselling-plr-products-2026-guide) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **$0 starting path:** Use Canva Free for design, Gumroad/Payhip for selling (no monthly fees), free AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) for content improvement. Validate demand via Pinterest/Reddit before creating. ([Strive With Jiya](https://strivewithjiya.com/how-to-start-a-digital-product-business-with-0-investment/) — accessed 2026-08-10; [Medium](https://medium.com/no-time/how-to-start-a-digital-product-business-with-no-audience-and-no-money-in-2026-63e8847aac55) — accessed 2026-08-10)
- **Realistic early earnings:** $50–$500/month within first few months. $200–$500/month is a common beginner benchmark. ([Online Money Guides](https://onlinemoneyguides.com/how-to-make-money-with-digital-products/) — accessed 2026-08-10)
