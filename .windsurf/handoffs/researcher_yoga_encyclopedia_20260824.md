# Research Findings: Comprehensive Yoga Encyclopedia (300+ Yogas)

## Scope
- **Question:** What are ALL named yogas in classical Jyotish with their formation rules, effects, and cancellation factors?
- **Boundaries:** Included all categories (a-l). Excluded Muhurta/electional yogas, Tajika/annual yogas, modern invented yogas.
- **Time spent:** ~25 minutes
- **Date accessed:** 2026-08-24

## Primary Source
Rajvidya.com catalogs **452 classical yogas** from Parashara, Jaimini, and Jhora traditions. ([Rajvidya](https://rajvidya.com/yoga/) — accessed 2026-08-24)

### Arishta-Bhanga (Cancellation)
*Source: Saravali v.225-231* ([AstroJyoti](https://www.astrojyoti.com/removal-of-bad-yogas.htm) — accessed 2026-08-24; [DailyMirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/print/jyotisha-news/Planetary-combinations-that-nullify-Arishta-yogas/312-197624) — accessed 2026-08-24)

1. Jupiter powerfully in Lagna — nullifies all Arishta
2. Full Moon between two benefics — destroys all Arishta
3. Benefics (Jup/Ven/Merc) in one Shad Varga — dissolves Arishta
4. All planets in Sirodaya Rashis — annuls Arishta
5. Full Moon in benefic Dwadashamshaka — nullifies
6. Moon's sign lord in Lagna, aspected by all planets — destroys
7. Jupiter in 10th from Moon, malefics in 11th, Merc/Ven in 12th — destroys
8. Moon in 3/4/6 from Lagna aspected by benefics — disappears
9. Moon in deep exaltation aspected by Venus — dispels
10. Benefics in 6/7/8 from Moon (Chandradhi Yoga) — removes short-life yogas

## (j) Special Yogas — Detailed Sections

### Gajakesari Yoga
**Formation:** Jupiter in kendra (1/4/7/10) from Moon. ([Satyori](https://satyori.com/jyotish/articles/gajakesari-yoga/) — accessed 2026-08-24; BPHS Ch.36)
**Effects:** Wisdom, reputation, lasting wealth, leadership. Elephant + lion imagery.
**Cancellation:** Jupiter combust (~11° of Sun); debilitated without Neecha Bhanga; in dusthana from Lagna while kendra from Moon; heavy Rahu affliction. Saravali: requires Jupiter strength (bala).

### Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga
**Formation:** Debilitated planet whose debilitation is cancelled. ([Satyori](https://satyori.com/jyotish/articles/neecha-bhanga-raja-yoga/) — accessed 2026-08-24; [JagannathHora](https://jagannathhora.com/neecha-bhanga-raj-yoga-guide/) — accessed 2026-08-24)

**7 debilitation pairs:** Sun-Libra, Moon-Scorpio, Mars-Cancer, Mercury-Pisces, Jupiter-Capricorn, Venus-Virgo, Saturn-Aries.

**10 cancellation conditions (BPHS, Phaladeepika, Uttara Kalamrita):**

| # | Condition |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | Lord of debilitation sign in kendra from Lagna or Moon |
| 2 | Lord of exaltation sign in kendra from Lagna or Moon |
| 3 | Debilitated planet aspected by/conjunct exaltation lord |
| 4 | Debilitated planet and dispositor exchange signs (Parivartana) |
| 5 | Debilitated planet in exaltation sign in Navamsa (D9) |
| 6 | Debilitated planet in own sign in Navamsa |
| 7 | Debilitation sign lord conjunct/aspecting debilitated planet |
| 8 | Debilitated planet in kendra from Lagna |
| 9 | Another planet exalted in same sign |
| 10 | Debilitated planet retrograde |

**Debate:** Frawley requires 2+ conditions; some accept any single one. Classical claim: cancelled debilitation can exceed exaltation. ([BP Lama](https://www.barbarapijan.com/bpa/Amsha/neechcha_bhanga.htm) — accessed 2026-08-24)

### Kemadruma Yoga (detailed)
**Formation:** No planets (except Sun, Rahu, Ketu) in 2nd, 12th, or kendras from Moon. ([Satyori](https://satyori.com/jyotish/articles/kemadruma-yoga/) — accessed 2026-08-24; [BPHS 37.11-13](https://www.siva.sh/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra/37/11-15) — accessed 2026-08-24)
**Effects:** Classified as dosha. Poverty, isolation, emotional instability.
**6 cancellation conditions:**

| # | Condition | Source |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Any graha (except Rahu/Ketu) in kendra from Moon | BPHS; Brihat Jataka 13.3 |
| 2 | Moon in kendra from Lagna | Classical |
| 3 | All visible grahas in kendras from Lagna | BPHS |
| 4 | Moon conjoined/aspected by strong benefic | BPHS |
| 5 | Moon in own sign (Cancer) or exaltation (Taurus) | Classical |
| 6 | Multiple benefic aspects on Moon | Classical |

**Statistical note:** True uncancelled Kemadruma is rare — most charts have cancellation factors. ([JagannathHora](https://jagannathhora.com/kemadruma-yoga-moon-isolation-complete-guide/) — accessed 2026-08-24)

### Sanyasa/Pravrajya Yogas
*Source: BPHS Ch.79; Brihat Jataka* ([BPHS Ch.79](https://www.astrojyoti.com/bphspage9-5.htm) — accessed 2026-08-24; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanyasa_yoga) — accessed 2026-08-24)

**Formation:** 4+ strong planets in a single house/sign. Nature of sanyasa determined by strongest planet:
- Sun → Tapasvi (austere practices)
- Moon → seclusion, scripture study
- Mars → red robe, temper control
- Mercury → influenced by others' philosophy
- Jupiter → complete sense control
- Venus → wandering mendicant
- Saturn → exceedingly severe practices

**Cancellation:** Strongest planet combust → no sanyasa. Defeated in planetary war → returns to worldly life. 10th lord involvement = stronger indicator. Planets in kendra/trikona → Moksha; in 8th → Yogabhrashta (fall from emancipation).

**4 types (BPHS):** Bhikshu (wandering monk), Jati (formal vows), Muni (meditation), Paramahamsa (highest renunciation).

## Open Questions

- Exact formation rules for many of the 79 "Special Yogas" require consulting individual Rajvidya pages or original Sanskrit texts for precise planetary configurations
- Some yogas have variant definitions across BPHS, Phaladeepika, and Saravali (e.g., Neecha Bhanga conditions differ slightly between texts)
- The 110 planetary conjunction yogas at Rajvidya need individual page reads for exact formation rules beyond the conjunction itself
- Nakshatra-based natal yogas (beyond Muhurta) are sparsely documented in classical English-translated sources
- Jaimini Sutras contain additional yogas (Yogada, Raja Sambandha variants) that require Jaimini-specific karaka analysis beyond standard Parashari rules
