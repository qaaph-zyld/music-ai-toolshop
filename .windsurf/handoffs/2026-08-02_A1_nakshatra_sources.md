## Summary

Researched all 27 nakshatras from three classical Jyotish sources (BPHS, Brihat Jataka, Phaladeepika) via web search. Produced three Markdown knowledge files with detailed entries for each nakshatra, including ruler, deity, shakti, symbol, gana, animal, pada-navamsa, mythology, effects, and classical references with citations.

**Key findings:**
1. **BPHS** does not have a dedicated natal nakshatra-effects chapter. Nakshatra deities are from ch.6 (Saptavimshamsa, sloka 24-26). Shaktis are from Taittiriya Brahmana I.5.1 (per Frawley). Gandanta effects are from ch.9.
2. **Brihat Jataka** natal nakshatra effects are in **Chapter XVI** (Rikshasiladhyaya), NOT Chapter 2 as originally specified. Chapter 2 covers planetary descriptions (Grahayoni prabheda).
3. **Phaladeepika** does not have a dedicated natal nakshatra-effects chapter. Chapter 4 is about Shadbalas (with Chandra Kriyas/Avasthas/Velas in sloka 16-20). Nakshatra transit effects are in Chapter 26 (sloka 35-40). The user's original specification of "chapter 4" was partially correct — ch.4 does contain Moon-based calculation systems derived from nakshatra positions.

## Gaps and Uncertainties

### 1. BPHS Natal Nakshatra Effects
**Gap:** BPHS does not contain a dedicated chapter describing natal personality effects for each nakshatra (unlike Brihat Jataka ch.16). The BPHS file uses shaktis and mythology from Taittiriya Brahmana I.5.1 (as translated by Frawley) and deities from BPHS ch.6 Saptavimshamsa. The "effects" described are derived from the shakti's nature rather than direct textual natal descriptions.
**Confidence:** High for deities (BPHS ch.6). High for shaktis (TB I.5.1 per Frawley). Medium for natal effects (inferred from shakti nature).

### 2. Brihat Jataka Chapter Reference
**Gap:** User specified "Brihat Jataka chapter 2" but the actual nakshatra effects are in Chapter XVI (Rikshasiladhyaya). Chapter 2 is "Grahayoni prabheda" (planetary descriptions). This has been corrected in the output file.
**Confidence:** High — the Brihat Jataka ch.16 text is directly translated and verified from multiple web sources.

### 3. Phaladeepika Chapter Reference
**Gap:** User specified "Phaladeepika chapter 4" but ch.4 is "Determination of Shadbalas." However, ch.4 sloka 16-20 do contain Chandra Kriyas/Avasthas/Velas (Moon-based calculation systems using nakshatra positions). The main nakshatra transit effects are in ch.26. Phaladeepika does NOT provide per-nakshatra natal personality descriptions.
**Confidence:** Medium for the framework. Low for per-nakshatra natal effects (not present in Phaladeepika).

### 4. Exact Verse Numbers
**Gap:** While chapter numbers have been verified, exact sloka/verse numbers within chapters may vary between Sanskrit editions and translations. The verse ranges cited (e.g., "sloka 24-26" for BPHS Saptavimshamsa deities) are based on the Santhanam translation. Other editions may have slightly different numbering.
**Confidence:** Medium — chapter references are solid; individual verse numbers may vary by ±1-2 between editions.

### 5. Pada-Specific Qualities
**Gap:** The pada-specific qualities listed in all three files are interpretive descriptions based on the navamsa sign ruler, not direct translations from classical texts. The pada-navamsa sign assignments themselves are well-established (from BPHS ch.6 navamsa calculation), but the personality qualities per pada are modern interpretive additions.
**Confidence:** High for navamsa sign assignments. Medium for pada personality descriptions (interpretive).

### 6. Yoni/Animal Assignments
**Gap:** The yoni/animal assignments are from the standard Jyotish tradition and are widely consistent across sources, but the exact classical textual source (BPHS vs. other texts) for these assignments could not be pinpointed to a specific verse. They appear in multiple commentaries and reference works.
**Confidence:** High for the assignments themselves (widely agreed). Medium for exact verse citations.
