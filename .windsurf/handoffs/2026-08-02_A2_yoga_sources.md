## Key Source Disagreements

### 1. Kemadruma Yoga Definition
- **BPHS**: No planet (except Sun) with Moon, in 2nd/12th from Moon, AND no planet in Kendra from Lagna. Cancellation: planet in Kendra from Lagna.
- **Phaladeepika**: No planet (except Sun) in 2nd/12th from Moon or conjunct Moon. Notes alternative Kendra-from-Moon view but considers it non-authoritative.
- **Saravali**: Broadest — absence of any of the three lunar yogas, OR Moon's angles devoid of planets, OR Moon unaspected by all planets. Cancellation: Moon's Kendras occupied or Moon aspected by all planets.

### 2. Pancha Mahapurusha Reckoning
- **BPHS**: From Lagna only.
- **Phaladeepika**: From Lagna AND Moon (explicitly stated in v.4).
- **Saravali**: From Lagna (Moon not explicitly mentioned for formation).

### 3. Chamara Yoga
- **BPHS Ch.36, v.11-12**: Lagna lord exalted in Kendra and aspected by Jupiter, OR two benefics in Lagna/9th/10th/7th.
- **Phaladeepika Ch.6, v.44**: Lagna occupied/aspected by benefic, Lagna lord not combust and in auspicious bhava in own sign/vargas.
- Different formation rules for the same yoga name.

### 4. Vipreet Raja Yoga
- **BPHS Ch.39, v.20**: Debilitated planets in dusthanas + strong Lagna lord aspecting Lagna. Does not use the term "Vipreet Raja Yoga."
- **Phaladeepika Ch.6, v.57-70**: Dusthana lords in other dusthanas produce Harsha, Sarala, Vimala yogas (positive effects from negative house lords).
- **Uttara Kalamrita IV.22**: The classic VRY definition — dusthana lords in other dusthanas, mutually related, not related to other lords.
- **Saravali**: Does not explicitly cover VRY as a separate category.
- **Disagreement on parivartana**: Some scholars argue that parivartana (exchange) between dusthana lords strengthens both houses and does NOT produce VRY.

### 5. Solar Yoga Categorization
- **BPHS**: Simple Vesi/Vosi/Ubhayachari; benefics give positive, malefics give contrary.
- **Phaladeepika**: Explicitly names Subhavesi/Papavesi, Subhavasi/Papavasi, Subhobhayachari/Papobhayachari.
- **Saravali**: Gives detailed planet-specific effects for each yoga (separate results for Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Mars, Saturn causing each yoga).

## Research Methodology

1. **Web searches** for each classical source and yoga type, targeting public domain translations.
2. **Multiple translation cross-verification**: Each yoga rule verified against at least 2 independent translations where available.
3. **Verse citations**: Exact chapter and verse numbers recorded from source texts. Where exact verses could not be confirmed, confidence marked as "medium."
4. **No fabrication**: Where exact verse numbers were uncertain, this was explicitly noted with reduced confidence levels.
5. **Source disagreements**: Explicitly documented where sources differ on formation rules, cancellation factors, or effects.
