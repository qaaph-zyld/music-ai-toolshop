## Key Disagreements Between Modern Authors

1. **Pancha Mahapurusha from Moon**: K.N. Rao follows Mantreswara (yes, from Moon also); B.V. Raman follows BPHS (Lagna only)
2. **7 vs 8 Karakas**: K.N. Rao firmly advocates 7; some Jaimini commentators use 8
3. **Karakamsha placement**: Rao places in birth lagna (North Indian method); others place in Navamsa chart (South Indian method)
4. **Chara Dasha year adjustments**: Rao omits exaltation/debilitation adjustments; some classical instructions include them
5. **Neechabhanga reliability**: Braha found it "nearly useless" in practice; classical texts and other modern authors treat it as valid
6. **Vargottama reliability**: Braha found Vargottama planets sometimes ineffective; most practitioners consider it a reliable strength indicator
7. **Vimshottari infallibility**: Braha states it is "fallible in a certain number of cases"; most practitioners treat it as the most reliable dasha
8. **Outer planets**: Braha advocates using Uranus, Neptune, Pluto; classical Jyotish and other modern authors do not
9. **Western aspects**: Braha integrates Western planetary aspects; other modern authors use only Vedic aspects
10. **Arudha Pada exceptions**: Rao does not apply classical exceptions; most classical texts prescribe them

## Gaps and Uncertainties

1. **K.N. Rao nakshatra analysis**: Rao's specific nakshatra interpretation methods are not extensively documented in available web sources. His focus is primarily on Jaimini techniques, PAC-DARES, and dasha systems.

2. **B.V. Raman Jaimini techniques**: Raman acknowledged Chara Dasha as "the emperor of Rashi Dasha systems" but did not publish extensive Jaimini technique documentation like Rao. His focus was primarily Parashari.

3. **Hart de Fouw Jaimini techniques**: "Light on Life" does not extensively cover Jaimini methods. De Fouw's focus is on Parashari principles, nakshatras, and Ashtakavarga.

4. **James Braha Ashtakavarga**: Braha does not extensively cover Ashtakavarga in his published works. His focus is on planet-in-house interpretations, dasha/bhukti experience, and remedial measures.

5. **Direct quotes**: All content is summarized from web research (book reviews, interviews, article excerpts, table of contents, chapter summaries).
